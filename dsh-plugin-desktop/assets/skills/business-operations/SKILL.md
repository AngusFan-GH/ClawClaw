# Business operations

Define the objective, owner, stakeholders, deadline, constraints, and evidence of success. Map the current process before prescribing a new one.

Describe work as explicit stages with inputs, outputs, decision rights, handoffs, service levels, and exception paths. Identify where information is duplicated, delayed, unavailable, or owned by nobody.

Use an action register containing owner, next action, due condition, dependency, risk, and completion evidence. Metrics must distinguish activity, output, quality, and business outcome.

Compare the status quo and proposed option on value, cost, time, risk, and reversibility. State assumptions and avoid fabricated budgets, headcount, dates, conversion rates, or commitments.

Deliver the decision required, operating plan, control points, unresolved dependencies, and first review checkpoint. Flag employment, financial, privacy, or contractual questions that require qualified review.
