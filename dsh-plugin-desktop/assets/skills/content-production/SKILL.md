# Content production

Create a brief before drafting: audience, desired action, channel, format, tone, length, evidence available, prohibited claims, and success signal. Ask only for missing information that would materially change the result.

Build the content around one clear promise. Use a structure appropriate to the channel, with specific examples and concrete language. Preserve factual meaning during localization or repurposing; adapt framing and length without inventing evidence.

Mark unsupported statistics, testimonials, legal claims, medical claims, product capabilities, and dates for verification. Do not fabricate quotations, customer stories, links, or performance results.

For multi-channel work, maintain a shared message spine and produce channel-specific variants. Record which elements must remain consistent and which may change. Include accessibility needs when relevant.

Deliver publishable copy plus a short fact-check list and required approvals. Separate editorial recommendations from confirmed platform behavior.
