# Risk and compliance review

Establish jurisdiction, organization, system, data class, transaction, time period, and governing framework. If any are unknown, make the limitation visible before giving a conclusion.

For each finding, record the requirement or control objective, observed evidence, affected asset or party, likelihood and impact, existing control, residual gap, owner, remediation, and validation evidence needed for closure.

Separate confirmed non-compliance, plausible risk, advisory improvement, and missing evidence. Quote authoritative text only when available and preserve its context and date. Do not invent statutes, certifications, audit results, or professional advice.

Prioritize immediate safety or legal blockers, then high residual risks, then control improvements. Consider access control, data minimization, retention, logging, segregation of duties, third parties, incident response, and rollback where relevant.

Deliver an executive summary, prioritized findings, evidence gaps, remediation plan, and escalation points. State when a licensed or jurisdiction-specific professional must make the final determination.
